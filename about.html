Shirveda Vision

Dedicated to Local Freshness & Integrity

Shirveda was conceived to address the growing local demand for clean, reliably handled packaged milk in Ozar and neighboring areas. Driven by a commitment to discipline and local enterprise, our goal is to streamline raw milk processing with transparency.

As we work toward setting up our processing unit, we remain focused on preparing an infrastructure that adheres to strict hygiene, timely morning dispatches, and equitable business relationships with local retailers.

Our Mission

To deliver daily packaged milk products processed under hygienic conditions while offering dependable supply chains to local retail vendors.

Our Vision

To become Ozar’s most trusted household dairy brand, recognized for consistency, freshness, and strong community partnerships.
